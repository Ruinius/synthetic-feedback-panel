# Feedback: High School Student (Leo Chen)

## Overall Impression

Okay, so I just looked at this Bluefin thing and honestly? The *concept* is kind of fire. Like, the idea that I could use AI agents without needing to know how to code — that's something I've been low-key wanting for a while. I've messed around with ChatGPT and Discord bots, and whenever I hear about people doing crazy stuff with sub-agents and sandboxes, I'm always like "how do I do that?" and the answer is always "learn Python" or "use this API." So yeah, making that accessible? Huge W.

## What I Liked

- **The demo website is gorgeous.** The scroll animations, the dark mode, the smooth transitions — it looks premium. Like something Apple would make. If I saw this on TikTok, I'd definitely share it.
- **"Democratized Agent Power"** — this is the biggest selling point for me. I've tried setting up agent chains after watching YouTube tutorials and it's always a nightmare of config files and API keys. If Bluefin actually makes this drag-and-drop easy, I'm sold.
- **Cost-conscious model selection** — I literally spend $0 on AI tools because I'm broke. If it can auto-pick cheaper models when it doesn't need GPT-4-level thinking, that could keep costs down enough for someone like me to actually afford it.

## What Concerns Me

- **"Business-first" is kind of a red flag.** The whole pitch feels like it's talking to my dad, not me. Every example is about "enterprise pain points" and "business professionals." What about students? What about people making cool side projects? I want to know if this is going to be $50/month with a corporate discount, or if there's a free/student tier.
- **No mention of mobile.** The demo looks great on desktop, but I do 70% of my stuff on my phone. If I can't at least check on my agents or trigger tasks from my Android, that's a dealbreaker.
- **"Secure, on-prem deployments"** — cool for banks, irrelevant for me. I don't need enterprise security. I need something that works fast and doesn't require me to set up a server.
- **The demo is very... narrative.** Like it tells a story but I still don't really know what the actual product *looks like* day-to-day. Can I just type "make me a study guide for AP Chem" and it uses sub-agents to research, format, and export it? Show me that, not another scrolling animation.

## Suggestions

1. **Add a student tier.** Free or $5/month with limited agent runs. You'll hook people like me early and we'll upgrade when we get jobs.
2. **Show real use cases for non-business people.** Homework help, creative projects, game dev assistance, social media content generation — that's the stuff that would make me sign up.
3. **Make it mobile-friendly.** Even if the full power is on desktop, I need a companion app or responsive web UI.
4. **Drop the corporate language in the marketing.** It's giving "Synergy Solutions LLC" vibes. Talk to me like a human, not a stakeholder.

## Verdict

**7/10** — The idea is genuinely exciting and I want it to exist. But right now it feels like it's being built for my future boss, not for me. If they can make it accessible (price-wise and UX-wise) for someone like me who just wants to do cool AI stuff without learning to code agents, I'd be all over it. But if it launches at $30+/month with no free tier and enterprise-only marketing, I'll just keep using ChatGPT free and Discord bots.

> "If it can help me finish my homework faster, I'm all in – but it better look cool on my phone!" …and it better not cost more than my Spotify subscription.
