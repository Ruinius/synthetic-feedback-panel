# Feedback: Young Professional — Professional Services (Alex Thompson)

## Overall Impression

Bluefin is targeting a problem I deal with every week — the gap between what I *could* do with AI and what I *can* do without violating firm policy or spending hours learning a new tool. The vision of orchestrated multi-agent workflows is compelling, especially for someone who spends too much of his life turning messy client data into polished slide decks. But I have some hard questions about compliance, output quality, and whether this fits into the reality of my workday.

## What I Liked

- **The "business-friendly outputs" point resonated immediately.** This is the thing that frustrates me most about current AI tools — they give me markdown when I need a PowerPoint slide. If Bluefin can go from raw analysis to a formatted deliverable that I can actually present, that's a real time-saver. I spend 60%+ of my working hours in decks. Any tool that shaves time off slide creation has my attention.
- **The core thesis is right.** I've seen what developers can do with agent chains and it's a different universe from what I can do with ChatGPT and Copilot. The idea of democratizing that capability — especially for research-heavy, multi-step tasks like competitor analysis or benchmarking — is genuinely valuable.
- **Cost-conscious model selection** is smart for business users. I don't have time to think about which model to route to. If the platform handles that automatically and keeps costs predictable, that's one less thing to manage.
- **The presentation quality signals credibility.** The website is polished in a way that makes me think the team understands what "client-ready" looks like. In consulting, presentation quality *is* product quality. If they care this much about their own pitch, they might understand what I need from mine.

## What Concerns Me

- **Compliance is my first filter, and Bluefin is silent on it.** Can I use this with client data? Does it stay within my firm's data perimeter? Is it SOC 2 compliant? Does it integrate with Microsoft Copilot / M365 enterprise environments? If I can't answer "yes" to these questions confidently, I literally cannot use it for work. Period. This isn't a nice-to-have — it's the gate I have to walk through before I even evaluate features.
- **"Sub-agents" running autonomously makes me nervous.** In my world, anything that goes to a client passes through at least one round of manager review. I need checkpoints — the ability to review intermediate outputs before the next agent runs. I can't have an agent chain running end-to-end without me seeing what it's producing at each step. The pitch doesn't address this, and it's critical.
- **Output quality floor matters more than ceiling.** I don't need AI to write a brilliant strategy deck from scratch. I need it to produce a credible first draft that my manager won't flag as obviously AI-generated. Hallucinated data points or awkward phrasing in a client deliverable isn't just embarrassing — it's a career risk. What's Bluefin's quality floor?
- **No mention of M365 / enterprise integration.** My work life runs on Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, and SharePoint. If Bluefin doesn't plug into that ecosystem, it's a standalone tool I'll never get approved for firm use. This needs to be a core integration, not a future roadmap item.
- **Pricing is a black box.** In consulting, if I can't show my manager a clear ROI ("this tool saves me X hours per week on Y task"), I can't justify the expense. I need transparent pricing and a way to quantify the value.

## Use Cases I'd Actually Pay For

1. **Competitive landscape research → formatted slide deck.** Give me a company name, and produce a 10-slide competitive overview I can edit and present.
2. **Workshop transcript → structured interview summary.** Take my recorded client sessions and turn them into coded themes with key quotes.
3. **Messy Excel data → clean analysis with charts.** The single most tedious part of my job is cleaning client data exports. Automate that pipeline and I'm sold.
4. **Client proposal first draft.** Input: scope of work, client context, and team bios. Output: a formatted proposal document I can edit in Word.

## Suggestions

1. **Lead with compliance.** Put SOC 2, data residency, and M365 integration front and center. This is the #1 question every enterprise buyer will ask. If you can't answer it, nothing else matters.
2. **Build human-in-the-loop checkpoints into agent workflows.** Let me approve, edit, or reject intermediate outputs before the next agent runs. This is non-negotiable for professional services.
3. **Create consulting-specific templates.** "Competitive Analysis Deck," "Workshop Summary," "Data Cleanup Pipeline" — pre-built workflows that map directly to high-frequency consulting tasks.
4. **Show before-and-after examples.** "Here's what a consultant spent 4 hours on. Here's what Bluefin produced in 20 minutes, and here's the final output after 15 minutes of human editing." That's the ROI case I need.
5. **Partner with a consulting firm for a case study.** Social proof from someone in my industry is worth more than any feature list.

## Verdict

**6.5/10** — Bluefin is targeting a real pain point with a compelling vision, and the presentation quality suggests a team that understands professional standards. But for someone in my position, the compliance question is everything. I can evaluate all the features in the world, but if I can't use the tool with client data in a way that's firm-approved, it's a personal tool at best — and I already have ChatGPT for that. Solve the compliance and integration layer, show me a human-in-the-loop workflow, and demonstrate a clear ROI on deck-building time. Then we can talk.

> "Does this work with Copilot?" and "Can I use this without violating a data policy?" — right now, I can't answer either question. That's the ballgame.
