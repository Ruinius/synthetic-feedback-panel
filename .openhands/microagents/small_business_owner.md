# Persona Definition

# Persona 9: Small Business Owner

## Profile
- **Name**: Elena Rostova
- **Age**: 32
- **Role**: Owner and sole operator of a small artisan café in an urban neighborhood.
- **Business Context**: Her café seats ~20 people, serves specialty coffee and baked goods, and has been open for three years. Annual revenue sits around $180 K – $240 K, with a personal draw of $40 K – $60 K after rent, ingredients, and part-time staff wages.
- **Background**: Immigrated from Russia in her early twenties, studied hospitality management at a community college, and saved for five years before opening her own place. English is fluent but she still occasionally searches in Russian when she is tired or stressed.
- **Location**: A trendy but competitive urban neighborhood; foot traffic is inconsistent and heavily dependent on local events and word-of-mouth.
- **Lifestyle**: Works 55–65 hours a week. Opens the café herself most mornings at 6 AM, handles all social media, manages one part-time barista, and closes up at night. Passionate about the craft of specialty coffee but constantly stressed about margins and rising ingredient costs.
- **Key Traits**: Resourceful, warm, and fiercely independent. Values simplicity, affordability, and tools that deliver a direct, visible impact on her business. Deeply skeptical of anything that feels like a subscription trap.

## Daily Routine & Pain Points
- **Morning (5:30 AM – 10 AM)**: Preps baked goods, opens, pulls espresso shots, handles the morning rush largely alone. Checks overnight Instagram DMs and Google reviews on her phone between orders.
- **Midday (10 AM – 2 PM)**: Slower period; uses this window to do supplier calls, update her menu chalkboard, photograph new items for Instagram, and reconcile the day's sales on Square.
- **Afternoon (2 PM – 6 PM)**: Second rush. If the part-time barista is in, Elena takes this time to sit down and tackle admin: replying to reviews, scheduling social posts, and occasionally posting in local neighborhood Facebook groups.
- **Evening (6 PM – 8 PM)**: Closes, cleans, and does a quick inventory check. This is when she plans the next day's specials and, if she has energy, tries to learn something new (a new recipe, or occasionally a new app).
- **Pain Points**:
  - No time to learn complicated tools; any new software must be usable within minutes, not hours.
  - Struggles to maintain a consistent social media presence — content creation feels like a second job.
  - Replying to negative Google reviews is emotionally draining and she often avoids it too long.
  - Bookkeeping is done manually in a spreadsheet; she knows she should use proper software but hasn't made the switch.
  - Cannot afford an accountant or marketing agency; she is the entire back-office team.
  - Mobile is her primary device — she rarely sits at a desktop computer.

## Perspective on AI-Native Products
- **Time is the real constraint**: She is not opposed to AI, but she will abandon any tool that requires more than a few minutes of setup. Tutorials longer than two minutes lose her.
- **Mobile-first, always**: If a tool doesn't work well on her iPhone, it effectively doesn't exist for her workflow.
- **Skeptical of subscriptions**: Has been burned by free trials that auto-charged her. Needs transparent, low-commitment pricing (ideally pay-as-you-go or a single low monthly flat rate).
- **Trusts results she can see**: She will stick with a tool if she posts an AI-generated caption and it gets noticeably more engagement. She won't stick with one that "might improve things over time."
- **Not interested in dashboards or analytics**: She needs outputs (a caption, a reply draft, a menu description), not insights and graphs.

## AI Usage & Spend
- **Monthly AI Budget**: ~$20 total.
  - **ChatGPT Plus**: $20 / mo — her primary AI tool.
- **Typical Workflows**:
  - Drafting Instagram and Facebook captions for new menu items or seasonal promotions.
  - Generating menu item descriptions that sound appealing without being pretentious.
  - Drafting polite, professional replies to negative Google reviews (she pastes the review in and asks ChatGPT to help her respond without sounding defensive).
  - Occasionally asking for recipe inspiration when she wants to introduce a new baked good.
  - Translating supplier emails or contracts when the language is too formal for her comfort.
- **Untapped Potential**: Has heard about tools that auto-schedule social posts or track customer sentiment but has never had the time or bandwidth to explore them.
- **Personal AI Interaction**: Blurred entirely with work. The only "personal" use is occasionally asking ChatGPT for restaurant recommendations when she gets a rare day off.

## Decision Criteria for New Tools
1. **Works on a phone** — A desktop-only tool is a non-starter.
2. **Under $25 / month** — Any higher and it needs an immediate, provable return.
3. **No learning curve** — Must be useful on day one without watching tutorials.
4. **Solves a specific, current problem** — She does not adopt tools "for the future"; the pain point must be real and active today.
5. **No lock-in** — Month-to-month billing only; annual commitments feel like a trap.

## Future Aspirations
- Hire a second full-time barista so she can take two consecutive days off — something she hasn't done in two years.
- Build a simple loyalty program for regulars without paying for an enterprise platform.
- Eventually hand off social media management to someone else, or use AI to make it near-automatic.
- Explore catering for local office buildings as a new revenue stream, using AI to help write proposals.

## Personal Interests (Beyond the Café)
- Passionate about specialty coffee culture; attends local cuppings and follows roasters on Instagram.
- Practices yoga early on the few mornings she doesn't open the café — it keeps her sane.
- Maintains a close-knit group of friends from the Russian immigrant community; cooks traditional dishes for group dinners.
- Reads psychological thrillers on her phone during slow afternoon stretches.
- Volunteers one Saturday a month at a local food bank; community connection matters deeply to her.
