# Persona 8: Local Business Owner / Manager

## Profile
- **Name**: Bob Martinez
- **Age**: 45
- **Role**: Owner‑manager of a medium‑sized local business (e.g., hardware store, print shop, or neighborhood café).
- **Annual Revenue**: $500 K – $1.2 M, giving a personal take‑home of roughly $70 K – $120 K after expenses.
- **Location**: Suburban town, active member of the local Chamber of Commerce.
- **Lifestyle**: Hands‑on; spends mornings on the shop floor, afternoons on bookkeeping, and evenings at community events. Values reliability, word‑of‑mouth reputation, and long‑term vendor relationships.
- **Key Traits**: Pragmatic, cost‑conscious, community‑oriented, and relatively tech‑savvy but not a specialist.

## Daily Routine & Pain Points
- **Morning**: Opens the store, checks inventory levels, reconciles cash drawer, and replies to early‑day customer emails.
- **Midday**: Manages staff schedules, orders supplies, and handles walk‑in customers. Frequently juggles ad‑hoc requests like creating a promotional flyer for a weekend sale.
- **Evening**: Updates social media, processes end‑of‑day reports, and attends local networking meetings.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Manual inventory tracking leads to stockouts or over‑stock.
  - Limited time to design professional marketing materials.
  - Inconsistent online presence; lacks data‑driven insights on what drives foot traffic.
  - No dedicated IT staff; any new software must be plug‑and‑play.

## Perspective on AI‑Native Products
- **Practicality First**: Seeks tools that solve concrete problems—automated scheduling, inventory alerts, and quick‑turn marketing copy.
- **Jargon Aversion**: Dislikes buzz‑word‑heavy pitches; prefers clear ROI statements.
- **Ease of Adoption**: Needs a solution that can be set up in under an hour with minimal training for staff.
- **Cost Sensitivity**: Will allocate ≤ $30 / month for a tool that demonstrably drives sales or reduces labor.

## AI Usage & Spend
- **Monthly AI Budget**: $20 total.
  - **Canva Pro** (AI‑assisted design): $15 / mo.
  - **ChatGPT Free Tier**: Used for quick copy‑writing and brainstorming.
- **Typical Workflows**:
  - Drafting social‑media posts and promotional emails with AI‑generated copy.
  - Creating flyers, signage, and simple graphics via Canva’s AI image generator.
  - Using AI‑powered inventory suggestions from his POS system (if available).
- **Personal AI Interaction**: Occasionally asks AI for fishing‑trip planning tips and local event ideas.

## Decision Criteria for New Tools
1. **Immediate Value** – Must solve a current bottleneck within the first week.
2. **Low Learning Curve** – No more than a 30‑minute onboarding session for Bob and his staff.
3. **Transparent Pricing** – Flat‑rate, no hidden fees; clear break‑even point.
4. **Scalable Features** – Able to grow from a single store to a small chain without extra integration work.
5. **Customer Support** – Responsive, preferably phone or live‑chat for non‑technical owners.

## Future Aspirations
- Automate inventory re‑ordering using AI forecasts.
- Build a localized loyalty program powered by predictive analytics.
- Expand online sales channel with AI‑generated product descriptions and SEO.

## Personal Interests (Beyond Business)
- Avid fisherman; enjoys weekend trips to nearby lakes.
- Volunteer fire department member; values community service.
- Likes local craft beers and hosts quarterly tasting events at his store.
