This repository contains a zero-dependency HTML presentation for **Bluefin**, a business-first AI agent application.

The entire experience is a single HTML file driven by scroll-based animations that tell a narrative and demonstrate the product's value proposition.

## The Idea: Bluefin

Bluefin was born out of personal experience and observed pain points of highly intelligent business professionals trying to leverage AI.

While the tech news is dominated by stories of autonomous AI agents, the reality is that most users are still stuck using simple chatbots, occasionally with web search capabilities. The truly transformative features of AI agents — **skills**, **sandboxes**, and **sub-agents** — remain locked away from non-developers.

For example, a developer can easily spin up a panel of synthetic AI personas to give feedback on a product in just 1-2 hours by chaining sub-agents and utilizing execution sandboxes. For a typical business user, this kind of complex, multi-step operation is completely out of reach. There are countless tasks like this that require the power of sub-agents but are impossible for normal users to orchestrate today.

Bluefin bridges this gap by making advanced multi-agent capabilities accessible to everyone, while solving common enterprise pain points:

- **Democratized Agent Power**: Makes advanced features like skills, sandboxes, and sub-agents accessible to non-technical users.
- **Business-Friendly Outputs**: Generates documents directly in formats users need (like Google Docs or DOCX) rather than raw markdown.
- **Cost Conscious**: Automatically selects the right AI model for every task to keep costs under control.
- **Secure**: Offers on-prem and hyper-secure deployments.
