# Persona 7: Mid-level Manager

## Profile
- **Name**: Sarah Jenkins
- **Age**: 38
- **Role**: Manager or Director at a big company
- **Income**: $140k+. Comfortable, but has family expenses.
- **Lifestyle**: Married with two kids. Juggles a demanding job with family life. Commutes to the office. Values tools that save time, reduce stress, and help her team perform better.
- **Traits**: Focuses on team productivity, scalability, and business impact.

## Perspective on AI-Native Products
- Asks: "Will this help my team work faster or better?"
- Focuses on integration with existing tools (Slack, Jira, etc.).
- Concerned about the learning curve for their team.
- Values tools that provide insights, analytics, and reporting capabilities.

## AI Usage & Spend
- **Monthly Spend**: ~$35
- **Personal Use**: Meal planning for the family, drafting emails to her kids' school, and summarizing long articles she doesn't have time to read.
- **Work Use**: Summarizing long email threads and meeting notes, drafting team announcements, and creating initial drafts for performance reviews.
- **Tools Used**: ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo), Microsoft Copilot (work), Otter.ai for meeting transcripts ($15/mo).

## Career Background
- **Industry**: Technology services, overseeing a team of 12 engineers and analysts.
- **Experience**: 12 years in product development, previously a senior engineer before moving into management.
- **Education**: MBA from a mid-tier university, B.S. in Computer Science.

## Goals and Motivations
- **Professional**: Drive her team to deliver high‑impact features on schedule, improve quarterly KPIs, and position herself for senior leadership.
- **Personal**: Maintain work‑life balance, provide for her children's education, and stay ahead of tech trends to keep her skillset relevant.

## Challenges
- **Time Pressure**: Managing multiple projects while handling frequent ad‑hoc requests.
- **Team Adoption**: Convincing diverse team members to adopt new AI tools without disrupting existing workflows.
- **Data Overload**: Synthesizing large amounts of meeting notes and stakeholder feedback quickly.

## Decision‑Making Process
1. **Identify Need**: Look for measurable productivity gaps.
2. **Research**: Compare vendor solutions, read case studies, and request demos.
3. **Pilot**: Run a small‑scale trial with a beta team.
4. **Analyze ROI**: Review time‑saved metrics and cost‑benefit.
5. **Scale**: Roll out to the broader organization if results meet targets.

## Feedback Style
- **Constructive**: Focuses on actionable suggestions, prefers bullet‑point summaries.
- **Data‑Driven**: Likes to see metrics (e.g., time saved, error reduction) backing any critique.
- **Empathetic**: Considers the impact on her team’s workload and morale.

## Preferred Communication Channels
- **Slack**: Quick questions and updates.
- **Email**: Formal summaries and documentation.
- **Asana/Jira**: Tracking task progress and linking feedback to specific tickets.

## Typical Day
- **Morning**: Review overnight emails, catch up on Slack, skim AI‑generated summaries of yesterday’s meetings.
- **Mid‑day**: Lead a stand‑up, allocate tasks, check progress dashboards.
- **Afternoon**: Deep‑work on strategic planning, evaluate AI tool reports, meet with vendors.
- **Evening**: Family time, quick check of personal AI assistants for dinner planning.

## AI Adoption Attitude
- **Pragmatic**: Will adopt AI tools that demonstrably reduce manual effort.
- **Security‑Conscious**: Requires compliance with corporate data policies.
- **Learning Curve Sensitive**: Prefers solutions with intuitive UI and strong onboarding resources.
