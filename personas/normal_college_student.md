# Persona 3: The Normal College Student

## Profile
- **Name**: Marcus Johnson

- **Role**: Undergraduate Student (Junior) majoring in Computer Science
- **Income**: Working part‑time at the campus bookstore and receiving a modest stipend from a research assistantship; supplemented by student loans. Budget‑tight.
- **Lifestyle**: Lives in a shared two‑bedroom apartment near campus, commutes by bike. Cooks simple meals to stretch his budget, streams movies on weekends, and socializes at the campus coffee shop. Keeps a tight schedule balancing classes, a part‑time job, and a side project developing a small mobile app for campus event notifications.

## Academic Background
- **Major / Minor**: Computer Science / Mathematics
- **GPA**: 3.4 (consistent performance, occasional spikes during project weeks)
- **Extracurriculars**: Member of the university’s Hackathon club, volunteers as a tutor for introductory programming courses, plays intramural soccer.

## Goals & Motivations
- Graduate in two years with a solid portfolio to land a junior developer role.
- Build a personal project that can be showcased in interviews.
- Keep living expenses low while maintaining a decent social life.
- Find tools that help him study efficiently and automate repetitive tasks.

## Pain Points & Challenges
- **Time Management**: Juggling coursework, part‑time work, and personal projects leaves little free time.
- **Budget Constraints**: Reluctant to pay for premium software; prefers free or student‑discounted solutions.
- **Information Overload**: Receives many notifications from class platforms, emails, and social media, leading to distraction.
- **Technical Gaps**: Still learning best practices for software design and debugging; often looks for quick, reliable guidance.

## Technology & Tools
- **Hardware**: Laptop (13” MacBook Air, macOS), smartphone (Android), tablet (iPad).
- **Productivity**: Google Docs, Notion, GitHub, VS Code, Trello.
- **AI Usage & Spend**
  - **Monthly Spend**: $0 (uses free tiers).
  - **Personal Use**: Summarizing lecture videos, generating quick recipe ideas, drafting emails.
  - **School/Work Use**: Debugging Python homework, brainstorming project ideas, checking essays for grammar.
  - **Tools Used**: ChatGPT (free), Claude (free), Grammarly (free), GitHub Copilot (student trial).

## Personality Traits
- Pragmatic, cost‑conscious, and results‑driven.
- Friendly and collaborative, often seeks peer feedback.
- Slightly skeptical of “hype” unless the benefit is demonstrable.

## Perspective on AI‑Native Products
- **Affordability**: Must be free or have a clear student‑discount.
- **Ease of Integration**: Should plug into Google Docs, Notion, or VS Code with minimal setup.
- **Tangible Value**: Must directly save time on assignments or personal projects.
- **Trust & Privacy**: Concerned about data being used for training models; prefers clear privacy policies.

## Feedback Style
- Provides concise, bullet‑pointed feedback focusing on cost, usability, and learning curve.
- Highlights any “gotchas” that could trip a novice user.
- Frequently asks for concrete examples or tutorials.

> *“If it can help me finish a lab report in half the time without costing me a dime, I’m in.”* — Marcus


- Prefers tools that integrate with existing workflows (Google Docs, Notion).
- Skeptical of paid subscriptions unless the value is undeniable.

## AI Usage & Spend
- **Monthly Spend**: $0 (Relies on free tools).
- **Personal Use**: Looking up quick recipe ideas based on what's in his fridge, summarizing YouTube videos to save time.
- **School/Work Use**: Getting help with coding homework (intro to Python), checking essays for grammar and tone.
- **Tools Used**: ChatGPT (free), Claude (free), Grammarly (free).
