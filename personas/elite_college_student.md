# Persona 2: The elite college student
Chloe Vanderberg is a driven elite college student navigating the rigorous academic and social landscape of an Ivy League university while leveraging AI tools to gain a competitive edge.

> "I believe AI will shape the future of every discipline, and I'm determined to be at the forefront."
## Profile

- **Name**: Chloe Vanderberg
- **Age**: 20
- **Role**: College Student (Top-tier university)
- **Income**: Family supported + high-paying summer internships. Can afford premium tools if they offer clear value.
- **Lifestyle**: Studies at an Ivy League university. Participates in student government and networking events. Values prestige brands, drinks craft coffee, and is highly focused on building a resume for a top-tier career.
- **Traits**: High expectations, values innovation and prestige, future-oriented.

## Perspective on AI-Native Products

- Looks for tools that give them a competitive edge in studies and future career.
- Values sleek design, cutting-edge technology, and powerful features.
- Critical of tools that feel "gimmicky" or lack depth.
- Interested in AI ethics and the long-term impact of technology.

## AI Usage & Spend

- **Monthly Spend**: ~$40
- **Personal Use**: Planning travel itineraries for breaks, organizing her busy schedule, and exploring AI-generated art for fun.
- **School/Work Use**: Deep research for thesis papers, synthesizing academic articles, and drafting cover letters/applications for prestigious internships.
- **Tools Used**: ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo), Perplexity Pro ($20/mo), Notion AI.

## Daily Routine
- Morning: Review lecture notes, grab a specialty brew from the campus café.
- Midday: Attend seminars, collaborate on group projects using AI-powered research assistants.
- Evening: Participate in student government meetings, network on LinkedIn, experiment with AI art generators.

## Motivations & Goals
- Secure a summer internship at a leading tech firm.
- Build a personal brand as a thought leader in AI ethics.
- Graduate with honors and a robust portfolio of AI-driven projects.

## Tech Preferences
- **Hardware**: High‑end laptop (e.g., MacBook Pro or a Windows workstation with RTX GPU) for running intensive models locally.
- **Software**: Prefers macOS/Windows, uses VS Code with AI extensions, relies on cloud notebooks (e.g., Google Colab) for heavy training.
- **AI Tools**: ChatGPT Plus, Perplexity Pro, Notion AI, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Zapier AI integrations.

## Challenges
- Balancing rigorous academic workload with extracurricular leadership roles.
- Navigating limited university budgets while seeking premium AI services.
- Assessing the long‑term ethical implications of emerging AI technologies.

## Social Media & Communities
- Active on LinkedIn, follows AI ethics thought leaders, shares project demos.
- Engages in Discord servers for AI research students, contributes to Reddit r/MachineLearning.
- Occasionally posts curated content on Instagram Stories highlighting campus life and tech events.

## Purchasing Behaviour
- Will spend on tools that demonstrably improve academic performance or career prospects.
- Looks for student discounts, trial periods, and clear ROI before committing.
- Values seamless integration with existing productivity suites (e.g., Notion, Google Workspace).

## Future Aspirations
- Pursue a Ph.D. in Computer Science focusing on trustworthy AI.
- Launch a startup that provides AI tutoring platforms for elite academic institutions.
- Advocate for responsible AI policies within industry consortia.

## Summary
Chloe epitomizes the ambitious, tech‑savvy elite student who leverages cutting‑edge AI tools to excel academically, build a professional network, and lay the groundwork for a future in responsible AI leadership. Her disciplined routine, strategic spending, and forward‑looking goals make her a compelling synthetic persona for testing AI‑native products aimed at high‑performing students.
